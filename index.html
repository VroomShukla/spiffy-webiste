01 — THE MARK

It starts as a line.

Before anything is built, it is drawn. A centerline, a grade, two footings — the first commitments an idea makes on paper.

02 — THE FRAME

Lines become structure.

Chords rise, decks land, bracing ties every panel together. Nothing is decorative — every member sits where it carries the most.

03 — THE MEASURE

Structure takes dimension.

Heights, spans, panel types, anchors. The sketch is checked and annotated until it becomes a plan a team can build from.

04 — THE DEPTH

A plan becomes a place.

The drawing stands up and takes its skin — stick by stick, wrapped in tape at every deck. The same structure, now real enough to touch.

PROGRAM B

Sacred Heart Nativity — engineering & entrepreneurship

SPIFFY's recurring program at Sacred Heart Nativity Schools. Middle-school students take one of two tracks — engineering or business — then bring both disciplines together to design, build, and pitch a real product.

THE CAPSTONE — WHERE THE TRACKS MEET

Two classes. One product.

In the capstone, engineering students and business students are combined into teams. Each team receives a theme — past themes have included stationery and household items — and spends roughly five weeks turning it into a physical product and a business case, presented Shark-Tank style in week ten.

STAGE 1 — SEPARATE FOUNDATIONS

THE PROCESS — SEVEN STEPS

  1. 01

    Separate foundations

    Engineering and business classes run independently — each track builds its own fundamentals first.

  2. 02

    Teams combined

    Students from both tracks are brought together into collaborative capstone teams.

  3. 03

    Theme received

    Each team develops a physical product around a shared theme — past themes include stationery and household items.

  4. 04

    Approximately five weeks of building

    Teams develop and physically build their products, applying engineering principles alongside budgeting and business decisions.

  5. 05

    Pitch prepared

    Each team prepares a business pitch covering how the product works and how the business would run.

  6. 06

    Week 10 — presentation

    Products are presented to parents, school staff, and other students, in a Shark-Tank-inspired showcase.

  7. 07

    The showcase

    Teams explain what they built, how it works as engineering, and how they would operate the business.
